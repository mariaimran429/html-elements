Mini Biography: Abdul Sattar Edhi

Abdul Sattar Edhi was a humanitarian, philanthropist, and social worker from Pakistan.

Early Life

He was born in Gujarat and grew up with compassion, simplicity, and a desire to help others.

Important Achievement
